Engagement Model

How I work

Not a menu of hourly tasks. A way of taking a business problem off your plate and bringing back a working product.

01

Discovery call

A 30-minute conversation about what you're trying to build, the constraints you're under, and whether this is a fit. No pitch deck.

02

Scoping & feasibility

Before anything is built, I map the business problem and check what's actually feasible — technically and financially — then scope budget, timeline and team.

03

Ownership through delivery

A small senior team delivers under my direction, in prioritized sprints with regular check-ins. One person to call, start to finish.

Engagement size and terms are discussed after the discovery call, scoped to what you're actually trying to build — not a fixed rate card.
