Tixters

Since Jun 2025

Built for a long-established US event promoter — Tixters itself is a newer platform, built from the ground up for their next event season.

They had no internal engineering team and needed a ticketing platform live before their next event season. I scoped the project, secured funding, and set the technical direction — then owned delivery from first brief through launch, with weekly client check-ins the whole way. What shipped is four apps on one shared backend: an organizer dashboard, an admin operations panel, buyer checkout, and door check-in, with Stripe Connect payouts, QR ticketing via SendGrid, sub-second door scanning, seat maps, and real-time sales analytics.

Live and processing real tickets within six months of kickoff. Zero downtime at live US events since. I've stayed on as technical lead and delivery owner.

Rails 7.2, GraphQL, PostgreSQL, Stripe Connect; React, Apollo, MUI

Feather Footwear

Since Sep 2025

Co-founded and Lahore-based. Women's sneakers, loafers and party shoes.

I co-founded this with a simple brief to myself: go from nothing to a live, sellable store fast, with paid acquisition that didn't bleed money from day one. I led the full digitalization — brand identity, store build, and paid media, in parallel — and set up server-side ad tracking before the first ad ever ran, so spend decisions had real data behind them from the start. Built as a mobile-first custom Shopify theme, since over 80% of traffic is mobile, with Meta Conversions API, Klaviyo flows, and product-level sales analysis feeding ad budget decisions.

First sale three days after launch. Profitable within four months, 8.7× Meta Ads ROAS. I'm still co-founder — the build and the business decisions both.

Shopify, Klaviyo, Meta Ads & Conversions API, GA4, Looker Studio
